The Reasoning

Why These Places

A note to ourselves — why each stop earned its place on the map.

Singapore

What a place to start. We've been many times, so this is simply a night to land, refresh and enjoy the lounge before the long haul on to Milan.

Milan

Really our gateway to Zagreb — but far too good to pass through blind. We'll take in some fantastic sights, above all The Last Supper, and we're looking forward to climbing the cathedral.

Zagreb

Somewhere we've never been, and I've heard only good things. There's a real food culture here, and by all accounts the city is extraordinarily beautiful. We can't wait.

The Croatia Cruise

We've met our fellow sailors, and now we're simply looking forward to the coast — the stops along the way, the snorkelling, the local cuisine, the wine and the beautiful weather.

Bilbao

Bilbao has long been on our radar. Deep in the Basque Country, the pintxos will go down very well — and we'll be meeting our friends from Cambridge, Paula and David, whom we first met in Porto last year.

Bordeaux

We wanted to explore the wine country slowly, arriving overland and giving ourselves four unhurried days. We can't look forward enough to the wine tour, the bike tour, and simply wandering beautiful Bordeaux.

Épernay · Champagne

Donna's paradise — and mine too, truth be told. This time we're staying longer than our brief visit last year, and giving ourselves properly over to the champagne houses and long lunches.

Antibes

We simply can't wait for the south of France. Friends who hail from here call it magical — and if it's anything like Nice, we're in for something special.

Naples

Somewhere we've passed through but never truly stopped. My father came from a town just outside the city, yet always considered himself a Napolitano — so this one is personal. We'll spend our days in a beautiful hotel overlooking the bay at Santa Lucia, and give ourselves over to the food.

Ischia

A longer, slower stay, and deliberately so. We've heard so much about Ischia, and unlike Capri or the Amalfi Coast it stays gloriously under the radar — exactly the unhurried, Italian kind of holiday we're after.

Monopoli · Puglia

Our base for the week. We'll fan out to the surrounding towns, but Monopoli was the obvious place to park ourselves and settle in.

The Gargano — Vieste & Monte Sant'Angelo

Chosen partly for its closeness to relatives near Foggia, but also because it's a breathtaking, under-talked-about corner of Italy we've long wanted to see.

Rome

Well — what can I say? The Eternal City, and we love it. We've extended our two days to fit a food tour with a chef who leads us to all the local treats. As an Italian, I can't wait.

Madrid

Officially our stopover to catch the flight home — but we love Madrid, and we're looking forward to the tapas and the colour and flavour of the city.

Hong Kong

Just one night, and a familiar one. We've stayed before, so it's really only a hop — but what a hop, in business class, on the way home.
